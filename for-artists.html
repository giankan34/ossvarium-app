WHAT IS OSSVARIUM?

OSSVARIUM is an independent archive built to help underground music travel beyond its usual circles.

It doesn't replace your Bandcamp, Spotify, YouTube, website or social media.

It gives people another way to discover your music — and leads them back to you.

WHY PI NETWORK?

Pi isn't the reason your music is here. The audience is.

OSSVARIUM lives inside the Pi ecosystem, opening underground music to a worldwide community of more than 60 million engaged Pioneers.

Someone who has never heard of your band can discover you through OSSVARIUM, listen to your music, follow your official links, share it outside the ecosystem and become part of your existing audience.

YOUR MUSIC. YOUR CONTROL.

Your music remains yours.

You decide what you submit, which tracks can be downloaded and how your release is presented.

OSSVARIUM does not ask you to abandon or replace any platform you already use.

DIRECT ARTIST SUPPORT

Listening is free. Downloads can be free when you allow them.

If someone wants to give something back, they can support an individual track or support you directly using Pi.
