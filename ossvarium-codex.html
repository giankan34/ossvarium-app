THE OSSVARIUM CODEX

The Official Archive Manuscript

I — THE PURPOSE

Not every album deserves to survive.

Some recordings disappear with time. Others become part of the history that created them.

The OSSVARIUM exists to preserve those rare relics whose significance has outlived their release.

What enters the Archive is not merely collected. It is remembered.

II — THE CURATOR

The Curator owns nothing. The Curator protects everything.

No relic enters the Archive because of price, rarity or reputation alone.

Each work is judged by its place within the underground, the history it carries and the mark it left behind.

The Curator does not decide what is valuable. Time has already done that.

III — THE ARCHIVE

The Archive is not a collection. It is a record of survival.

Every preserved relic receives its own identity, archive record and certificate of preservation.

Its origin, history and significance are recorded so that what once existed in the underground is not lost to time.

Once preserved within the OSSVARIUM, the relic becomes part of the eternal Archive.
