PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 15, 2026

OSSVARIUM is an independent underground music archive, discovery and artist-support platform.

We respect the privacy of artists, listeners and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you interact with OSSVARIUM.

1. DATA CONTROLLER AND CONTACT

OSSVARIUM is an independent music platform currently operated by its founder as an individual, rather than a registered company.

OSSVARIUM is operated by its founder, Ioannis Kannas, who acts as the data controller for personal data processed through the platform.

For privacy inquiries and data protection requests, contact: contact@ossvarium.com

2. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

Depending on the features used, OSSVARIUM may process:

  • Artist names, band names, contact email addresses and submitted artist biographies.
  • Release information, artwork, track metadata and other content submitted for publication.
  • Email verification codes, authentication records and information needed to manage artist access.
  • Pi username, Pi user identifier or other account information authorized through Pi authentication.
  • Track purchases, artist-support records, payment identifiers and payout requests where those features are available.
  • Messages and information voluntarily provided when contacting OSSVARIUM.
  • Technical information necessary for security, troubleshooting and platform operation.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Personal information may be used to:

  • Review and publish artist submissions.
  • Verify artist identity and provide access to artist management features.
  • Maintain releases, profiles and music collections.
  • Process supported transactions and maintain relevant records.
  • Respond to questions, support requests and removal requests.
  • Protect the platform against abuse, unauthorized access and fraudulent activity.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of OSSVARIUM.

4. LEGAL BASES FOR PROCESSING

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, personal information is processed only where an appropriate legal basis exists.

Depending on the purpose, these bases may include performance of an agreement with the user, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests in operating and securing the platform, or consent where required.

Where processing relies on consent, users may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

5. ARTIST SUBMISSIONS AND PUBLIC CONTENT

Artist names, release titles, biographies, artwork, music and selected external links may be publicly displayed when submitted and approved for publication.

Contact email addresses provided for account verification or administrative communication are not intended to be displayed publicly unless separately authorized by the relevant person.

Artists may request corrections or removal of their information by contacting OSSVARIUM.

6. EMAIL VERIFICATION

OSSVARIUM may use email verification codes to confirm access to artist-management features.

Verification-related information is processed for authentication and security purposes. Email delivery may be handled by an external email service provider.

7. PI NETWORK FEATURES

OSSVARIUM may use Pi authentication, Pi payment functionality and related Pi ecosystem services.

When users authorize these features, OSSVARIUM may receive account identifiers and transaction information necessary to provide the requested functionality.

The Pi ecosystem operates under its own applicable terms and privacy practices.

8. PAYMENT AND TRANSACTION RECORDS

Where payment or artist-support features are enabled, OSSVARIUM may maintain records of transaction identifiers, purchased tracks, amounts, artist earnings and payout status.

Such records may be required for account functionality, security, dispute resolution, accounting and legal compliance.

Testnet transactions are experimental and should not be confused with live commercial payments.

9. LOCAL STORAGE AND COOKIES

OSSVARIUM may use browser local storage and similar technologies to maintain session information, user preferences, collection progress, achievements and other application features.

Some technologies may be necessary for authentication, security and essential platform functions.

Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, such technologies should not be activated before valid consent has been obtained.

10. SERVICE PROVIDERS

OSSVARIUM uses third-party infrastructure and technical services to operate the platform, including:

  • Vercel — website hosting and server functions.
  • Neon PostgreSQL — database services.
  • Amazon S3 — storage of uploaded music and related files.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery and verification.
  • Cloudflare — DNS and email routing.
  • Pi Network — authentication and Pi-related features.

Providers may process relevant information only for their applicable service purposes and subject to appropriate contractual and legal safeguards.

11. DATA SHARING

OSSVARIUM does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed to service providers when necessary to operate the platform, comply with law, protect legitimate rights or respond to valid legal requests.

12. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Some service providers may process or store information outside the European Economic Area.

Where GDPR restrictions apply to international transfers, appropriate legal transfer mechanisms and safeguards must be used.

13. DATA RETENTION

OSSVARIUM respects the principle of full artist control. Artists may request the permanent removal of their profiles, releases, music, artwork and associated personal information at any time.

Upon a verified deletion request, OSSVARIUM will remove the relevant content and personal data from its active systems, including its database and file storage, without undue delay.

Limited information may be retained only where required by law or necessary for legitimate legal or security purposes. Residual copies in technical backups will be removed according to applicable backup retention procedures.

Other personal information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Deletion requests may be sent to contact@ossvarium.com

14. SECURITY

OSSVARIUM aims to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration and misuse.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

15. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Where the GDPR applies, individuals may have rights to access, correct or erase their personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and request data portability where applicable.

Individuals may also have the right to withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

Privacy requests may be sent to contact@ossvarium.com .

16. THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES

OSSVARIUM may link to Bandcamp, Spotify, YouTube, artist websites and other independent services.

Their own terms and privacy policies apply when users access those services.

17. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

OSSVARIUM is not specifically designed for children. Additional legal requirements may apply where personal information relating to minors is processed.

18. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated as OSSVARIUM develops and its features change.

Material changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.

19. CONTACT

For privacy questions, access requests, corrections or deletion requests, contact:

contact@ossvarium.com
