PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

OSSVARIUM is an independent underground music archive and discovery platform built for the Pi ecosystem.

1. INFORMATION WE MAY PROCESS

When Pi authentication or other Pi Platform features are used, OSSVARIUM may process information made available through those services, such as a Pi username or other information authorized by the user.

Some application features may also store information locally on your device, including collection progress, achievements, preferences and archive progression.

2. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information is used only to operate and improve OSSVARIUM, provide application features, maintain user progress and support functionality within the platform.

3. LOCAL STORAGE

OSSVARIUM may use browser local storage to remember features such as collected relics, XP, achievements and other application progress.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

OSSVARIUM may provide links to external music platforms, artist websites and other third-party services. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit those services.

5. PI NETWORK

Features involving Pi authentication or Pi transactions may rely on services provided by the Pi ecosystem. Information processed by those services is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. DATA SHARING

OSSVARIUM does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the application.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated as OSSVARIUM develops and new features are introduced.

8. CONTACT

Questions concerning this Privacy Policy may be directed to the OSSVARIUM developer through the contact information provided by the application.
